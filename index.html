Privacy Policy for Fruit Splash

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Last Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fruit Splash ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you install, play, or interact with our mobile game on Android devices through the Google Play Store.

By downloading, accessing, or playing Fruit Splash, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue using the application.

2. Information Collection and Storage

A. Information We Do NOT Collect

Fruit Splash does not require user registration, account creation, or login. We do not ask for or collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, email address, phone number, biometric data, or financial payment details.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide smooth gameplay, your game progress is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences/DataStore. This includes:

  • Levels unlocked (1 through 20) and highest level reached
  • Stars earned per level and high scores
  • In-game virtual coin balance
  • Audio and vibration preference toggles (Sound On/Off, Haptics On/Off)
  • Daily bonus claim timestamps

This data remains strictly on your device and is not transmitted to our personal servers.

C. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties (AdMob)

To support free gameplay, Fruit Splash integrates Google AdMob to display Banner ads, Interstitial ads, and Rewarded video ads. Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect certain diagnostic and non-personal device information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address (anonymized/coarse location), device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and system language.
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Impressions served, ads viewed, clicks, completion status of rewarded ads, and ad response latency.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: App crash logs, system diagnostics, and ANR (Application Not Responding) metrics.

5. Device Permissions Used by the App

Fruit Splash requests only the minimum essential Android system permissions required for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the game to connect to the internet to load banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements from Google AdMob.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the AdMob SDK to check whether an active Wi-Fi or cellular network connection is available prior to requesting ads.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Allows the app to provide satisfying haptic feedback when linking fruit chains and matching combos (can be toggled off at any time in the in-game Settings).

The app does not request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, or External Storage.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Fruit Splash is an all-ages family casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

All advertisements requested by the app comply with Google Play's Families Policy and Better Ads Standards. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at kunakom01@gmail.com, and we will promptly take steps to remove such information.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Choices (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

A. Personalized Advertising Opt-Out

You can opt out of personalized advertisements or reset your Google Advertising ID on your Android device at any time by navigating to:

Android Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

B. Clearing Local Game Data

Because all game data (stars, scores, unlocked levels, and coins) is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can delete all stored data at any time by:

  • Selecting the Reset Progress button in the in-game Settings dialog; OR
  • Navigating to Android Settings → Apps → Fruit Splash → Storage → Clear Data; OR
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

C. Rights for EEA and California Residents

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data. Because we do not collect personal identifiers, ad-related requests should be directed to Google through their dedicated ad privacy tools.

8. Data Security

We value your trust in using our app. Although no method of electronic transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, all communication with third-party advertising networks (Google AdMob) is conducted using industry-standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL / HTTPS) encryption.
